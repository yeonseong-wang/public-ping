# Ping
When your Wi-Fi connection keeps dropping, use Ping on your iPhone to see how quickly your router responds. Enter an IPv4 address or domain name to send requests directly to the device you want to check and measure its response time in milliseconds.

## Check your connection
Enter the address of a router or server and tap **Start** to send one ping per second. The log shows the round-trip time (RTT) for each reply. If a request goes unanswered for two seconds, the log shows a timeout. Tap **Stop** at any time to review your results.
- **Packet loss** shows how often requests time out. You can also see how many packets you sent and how many replies you received.
- **Minimum, average, and maximum RTT** show how much response times varied during the session.
- **UTC timestamps** help you match a slow reply or timeout to the moment you noticed a problem. Tap **Copy** to paste up to 1,000 of the most recent log lines into an incident report.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
