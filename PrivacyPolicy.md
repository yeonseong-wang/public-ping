# Ping Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-11-24
Developer: YeonSeong Wang
Ping lets you check whether an IPv4 address or domain responds to a ping request. We do not collect or store your personal information or diagnostic logs. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

### Network requests
When you enter a domain name, Ping uses your device’s DNS service to look up its IPv4 address.
The DNS service receives the domain name you enter.
Ping then sends requests directly to the target you select.
The target can see the source IP address of those requests.
To reach devices on your local network, Ping may ask for Local Network access.
You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

### Diagnostic logs
Ping keeps up to 1,000 recent log entries in the app’s memory.
Entries may include the target address, UTC date and time, response times, and timeouts.
Starting a new ping session clears the previous session’s log.
If you tap Copy, Ping copies the current log to your device’s clipboard.
